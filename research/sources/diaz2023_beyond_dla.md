## Claim (currently paraphrase-level, needs follow-up full-text quote)
For parametrized matchgate (free-fermionic) circuits, when the LASA condition (O∈i𝔤 or ρ∈i𝔤)
of Ragone et al. is relaxed -- i.e. observable/state genuinely outside the DLA -- "the variance
does not necessarily vanish inversely with the Lie algebra's dimension. Instead, this measure
of expressiveness is replaced by a generalized expressiveness quantity: the dimension of the
Lie group modules."

## Why this matters for fracture framing
This is exactly the open sub-case flagged in Ragone et al.: the general theorem (Candidate 1)
is proved only for O/ρ ∈ i𝔤. Diaz et al. solve the relaxed case for ONE narrow, exactly-solvable
ansatz family (matchgates/free fermions, itself classically simulable via Jordan-Wigner --
so not even a "hard" QML setting). The general relaxation -- non-free-fermionic ansatz classes,
generic observables outside the DLA -- remains open. This is the gap.
